What the contract will and will not do

These are the rules in contracts/bandlot.py. The worked numbers are the public DE-LU print for 12 Nov 2026, interval 0, which was 224.90 EUR/MWh.

The print is fetched

The URL is api.energy-charts.info/price for one zone and one date. Validators fetch it again. A typed euro price is not an argument.

Markup, then floor

224.90 at +100 bps becomes 227.14 EUR/MWh. At +400 bps it becomes 233.89. The remainder is dropped, not rounded up.

Price-time match

Same zone, date, and interval. Seller markup at or under the buyer's cap. Full size unless both sides allow a partial. Lowest markup wins. Ties go to the earlier ask.

Escrow is computed

The buyer attaches the wei quote returns. One euro-cent of indexed notional is 1012 wei on this book. That scale is not a claim about the price of GEN.

Clear pays, and refunds the rest

A 100 bps ask against a 400 bps bid pays the 227.14 figure and returns the difference. No match, no transfer. The seller cannot take their own bid.

Fail closed

Unreadable, rate-limited, wrong unit, or a series that is not 92, 96, or 100 quarters does not pin. A zero or negative interval cannot be posted. This is not a delivery of electricity.

Day-ahead figures are read from energy-charts.info. The DE-LU series used in the worked example is published there as CC BY 4.0 from Bundesnetzagentur | SMARD.de. Bandlot stores integer euro-cents and settles a GEN notional. It does not schedule generation.
